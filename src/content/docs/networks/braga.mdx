<Aside type="caution" title="Braga was retired on 12 August 2026">
  Braga was retired at 23:59 CET on 12 August 2026. There are no public endpoints and no faucet. [Tiramisu](/networks/tiramisu/) is the current public testnet.
</Aside>

See the [retirement announcement](https://x.com/arkivnetwork/status/2082409854308626490) for details.

## What to do now

- **Build on Tiramisu.** See the [Tiramisu page](/networks/tiramisu/) for the chain ID, RPC endpoints, and faucet.
- **Move an old app forward.** Entities do not carry over between networks, and SDK v0.8 has breaking changes. See [Older networks](/networks/older-networks/).
