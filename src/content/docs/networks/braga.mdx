<Aside type="caution" title="Braga shuts down 12 August 2026">
  Braga shuts down at 23:59 CET on 12 August 2026. After that there are no public endpoints and no faucet. An experimental devnet is running with limited access, and a public testnet follows in September.
</Aside>

See the [shutdown announcement](https://x.com/arkivnetwork/status/2082409854308626490) for details.

## What to do now

- **Want into the devnet?** An experimental devnet is running in August with limited access. Just ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/arkiv) and we'll get you set up.
- **Waiting for the next testnet?** A public testnet opens in September 2026. Watch Discord for the announcement, and check back on this page.
