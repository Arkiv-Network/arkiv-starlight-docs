## Moving an old app forward

Entities don't carry over between networks. Testnet state isn't ported, so anything you wrote to Kaolin stayed on Kaolin, and anything you wrote to Braga stayed on Braga.

Tiramisu runs on SDK v0.8, which has breaking changes from v0.7. Pointing your app at the new chain is not enough on its own. Start from the [Installation](/start-here/installation/) page and the [TypeScript SDK](/typescript-sdk/querying-data/) guides, which are written for v0.8.
